Signing in

Use the same email you used to register your player on way2gosports.com. We'll email you a 6-digit code — enter it to sign in. If no code arrives in a few minutes, check your spam folder and make sure you used the exact email from your registration. Still stuck? Email us and we'll help match your account.

I don't see my child's team

Teams appear in the app once the league publishes them for your season. Before that, you'll see a "You're in!" screen confirming your registration. Pull down to refresh, or reopen the app, once teams are announced.

Notifications

Turn categories (coach updates, team chat, league alerts) on or off under the Profile tab. You can also manage notifications for the app in your device's Settings app.

Reporting or blocking a message

Tap the flag next to any chat message to report it to Way2Go or block the sender. Blocking hides that person's chat messages for you; official coach announcements stay visible. Manage your blocks under Profile.

Deleting your account

Open the Profile tab and tap "Delete account." This removes your app sign-in and notification devices from our systems. Your league registration and payment records are kept as business records, as described in our policies on way2gosports.com.
