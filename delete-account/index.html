Way2Go! Sports

Delete your app account

You can request deletion here, even if you no longer have the app.

Request deletion by email

Send your request from the email address you use to sign in to Way2Go! Sports. Use the subject Delete my Way2Go app account.

Email a deletion request

If the button does not open your email app, write to dustinway2go@gmail.com. Please do not send passwords, sign-in codes, or payment card details.

We will confirm ownership of the account before processing a request and confirm when it is complete. If you cannot use your sign-in email, contact us for help.

Delete from the app

Open Profile → Delete account, review the confirmation, and choose Delete account. The app signs you out after the server confirms deletion. If an error appears, your request has not been confirmed; retry or contact us.

What is removed

Deletion removes your app sign-in sessions, notification devices, calendar access, personal app settings, caregiver sharing links, app access requests, and messages and chat photos you posted. Existing app and parent-browser sessions lose access. Other family members keep their own accounts.

What remains

  • League records. Registration and payment records remain under the league's website policies. Scheduled team events remain, and some operational attendance and notice records may remain so the league can continue operating.
  • Safety records. Moderation outcomes and a hashed account identifier may remain to preserve other people's blocking choices and prevent old sign-in sessions from regaining access.
  • Recovery copies. Encrypted disaster-recovery backups may persist after deletion. They are separate from the active app and are not available to families.
  • Copies already shared. A recipient may have saved a message, photo, or notification outside the app. Account deletion cannot remove those copies from their device.

Deleting your app account does not cancel a league registration, withdraw a player, or request a refund. Contact Way2Go separately for help with a registration or league record.
