Privacy Policy

Way2Go! Sports Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Summary

  • The Way2Go! Sports app is a companion for families already registered in our youth sports league. You sign in with the email you used to register.
  • We use your information to run the league, show your child’s team and schedule, deliver team messages, and manage your membership if you join League Supporter.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can sign out or delete your app account at any time from the Profile screen.

Who We Are

Way2Go! Sports is a family-run youth sports league in the Santa Clarita Valley, California. This policy covers the Way2Go! Sports mobile app. Registration, payments, and league records on our website (way2gosports.com) are covered by the policies on that site.

Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Sign-in email. You enter the email address tied to your league registration. We email a one-time code to confirm it's you. We do not store a password.
  • Messages you send. Team chat messages and coach announcements you post are stored so your team can read them.
  • Family access and support. We store caregiver names, email addresses and sharing permissions you provide, along with requests for help linking a child or correcting app access. Include only the details the league needs to help.
  • Team-update confirmations. We record which update you viewed or confirmed and the child it applies to so coaches can follow up accurately.
  • RSVPs. If you mark whether your player is attending a game or practice, we store that response for your team.
  • Photos you attach in team chat. If you choose to share a photo, the app uses the system photo picker, so we only receive the specific photos you pick, never your whole library. Photos are re-encoded before storage, which removes hidden metadata such as GPS location, and photos from parents are reviewed before the rest of the team can see them.

Information from your league registration

  • To show you the right team, we display information already on file from your registration: your player's name, team, division, and season, and your contact name. The app reads this; you enter it during registration on our website, not in the app.

Information collected automatically

  • Push notification token. If you allow notifications, Apple on iOS or Google Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android provides an app-specific device token that we associate with your sign-in email so we can send you team and league alerts. It is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • The app keeps a copy of your latest team and schedule data on your device so it works quickly and offline. That copy stays on your phone.

Optional drive-time estimates. With your permission, the iPhone app uses your current location to request a drive-time estimate through Apple Maps. Way2Go's backend does not receive or store those coordinates. You can turn off location permission in your device Settings and still view your family's schedule.

We do not access your contacts, your whole photo library, or an advertising identifier.

Payments

Families can use the app for free. League Supporter is an optional purchase. The app can display registration amounts, payment status, and receipt references already held by the league. It does not collect full card numbers or payment security codes. Registration purchases are handled through the league website and its payment providers.

League Supporter Subscription

League Supporter is an optional monthly membership. Your badge is visible to your team in chat.

Who handles payment. Apple handles in-app subscriptions. Where web checkout is offered, we send your sign-in email to Stripe to create your billing account. You enter payment details directly with the provider. Way2Go does not receive your full card number.

What Way2Go keeps. We link billing records to your Way2Go account so we can show your team-chat badge and help with renewals, cancellations, and billing questions. Records include provider identifiers for purchases or subscriptions, subscription status, and billing dates. Stripe also supplies customer and invoice identifiers.

Where to manage billing. The League Supporter section in Me opens billing for web purchases. Apple purchases are managed in your Apple Account subscriptions. Cancel renewal before deleting your Way2Go account. For help finding a membership, contact Way2Go with the purchase email and receipt date.

How We Use Your Information

  • To sign you in and show you your family's teams, schedules, standings, and game results.
  • To deliver team chat, coach announcements, and league alerts, and to send push notifications you've turned on.
  • To keep the league safe: for example, letting you report or block a chat participant, and letting coaches remove inappropriate messages.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the app.

How Information Is Shared

  • With your team. Messages you post and RSVPs you make are visible to the parents and verified coaches on that team. That is the purpose of team chat.
  • Service providers. We use Apple Push Notification service on iOS, Google Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android, and an email provider for one-time sign-in codes. They process this data only to provide those services.
  • We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of our participants.

Your Choices

  • Notifications. Turn categories on or off in the app's Profile screen, or disable them entirely in your device Settings.
  • Blocking. On iPhone, use the flag next to a message and manage blocks under Profile. On Android, press and hold a message to block its sender; contact support if you need to undo a block.
  • Sign out. Sign out from the Profile screen at any time.
  • Delete your account. Use "Delete account" on the Profile screen, or request deletion without the app. Review the deletion and retention details below before confirming.

Children's Privacy

The Way2Go! Sports app is intended for parents, guardians, and coaches, not for children. Players do not create app accounts. A player's name and team are entered by a parent during registration so the family can see their own schedule. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 through the app.

Account Deletion and Data Retention

When the server confirms account deletion, we remove your app sessions, notification and calendar tokens, personal app preferences, caregiver sharing links, app access requests, and messages and chat photos you posted. Existing app and parent-browser sessions lose access. Deletion does not remove another family member's account.

League registrations and payment records remain under the policies on way2gosports.com. Scheduled team events remain, and some operational attendance and notice records may remain; personal app attribution is removed where it is no longer needed. Deleting the app account does not cancel a registration, withdraw a player, or request a refund.

We may retain moderation outcomes and hashed account identifiers to preserve other people's blocking choices and revoke old sessions. These safety records are not used for advertising. Other people's messages and independent safety reports are their records; deletion removes your authored content and direct identity from the affected app records.

Revoked browser-session records expire under our seven-day session policy or have their parent-account fields cleared on next use. Encrypted disaster-recovery backups are kept separately from the active app and may persist after deletion. Daily backups rotate, while monthly and release snapshots can remain longer for recovery. Copies already saved by another person, or alerts already delivered to their device, are outside the app's control.

To request account deletion without reinstalling the app, use our deletion request page. Contact dustinway2go@gmail.com with questions about retained league records or recovery copies. We confirm ownership before processing an email request and confirm when it is complete.

Tracking and "Do Not Track"

The app contains no advertising or analytics trackers, and no third parties collect personal information about your activity across other apps or websites through the app. Because we do not track you, the app behaves the same whether or not your browser or device sends a "Do Not Track" signal.

Security

Sign-in uses a one-time emailed code and a device-held token rather than a stored password. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email dustinway2go@gmail.com or visit way2gosports.com/contact.
